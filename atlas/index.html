Questions worth asking

No theatre. Just the operating model.

Who owns the customer relationship?

The franchisee. ATLAS and TravelOS are infrastructure around the relationship, not a replacement for the advisor.

Does ATLAS replace a travel advisor?

No. It handles structured intake and operational handover so the advisor can spend more time on judgement, selling and service.

Is supplier inventory already live?

The platform is designed around provider integrations. Live inventory, pricing and availability should only be presented when a configured supplier source supports them.

Is this an AI-only travel business?

No. AI-assisted workflows are one part of the stack. Customer trust, commercial judgement and service remain human responsibilities.
